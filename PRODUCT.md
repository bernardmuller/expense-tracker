# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual consumers in South Africa tracking personal budgets and expenses. Mobile-first users on phones, managing daily spending, setting budgets, and monitoring financial health through an app interface.

## Product Purpose

A personal finance tracker that makes budgeting feel accessible and engaging, not tedious. Users set budgets, track expenses, categorize spending, view charts, and manage recurring expenses. Success is users actually sticking to their budgets and feeling in control of their money.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bold, warm. The app should feel like a friendly companion that makes finance less intimidating. Energetic without being childish. Confident without being cold.

## Anti-references

- Cold institutional banking UI (sterile blues, corporate stock imagery)
- Generic SaaS dashboards (cream-on-cream, numbered section eyebrows, gradient text)
- Spreadsheet-like financial tools (dry data tables with no personality)
- Typical fintech apps that feel like Bloomberg terminals

## Design Principles

1. **Finance should be fun** — Make money management feel like a game, not a chore
2. **Mobile-first confidence** — Every interaction should feel native and effortless on a phone
3. **Bold clarity** — Use color, weight, and size to create hierarchy without confusion
4. **Warmth over sterility** — Approachable palettes and rounded shapes, never cold or institutional
5. **Celebrate progress** — Visual feedback for milestones and positive financial behavior

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance target
- Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Color contrast ratios maintained for text on colored backgrounds
- Semantic HTML and ARIA attributes on interactive elements
